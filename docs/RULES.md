# Rules contract

Source verified 2026-10-16: publisher-authored core rulebook downloaded from https://www.brotherwisegames.com/s/NightoftheNinjaCoreRulebook.pdf and official FAQ https://www.brotherwisegames.com/s/NightOftheNinjaFAQ.pdf. Rules summaries and card text here are independently phrased; no publisher artwork or branding is used.

33 action cards: six numbered 1–6 in each of Informant, Oracle, Cutthroat and King's Blade; six unique Conspirators numbered 1–6; two reactions and one Dawn card. Original-to-themed mapping is recorded in shared/cards.js for auditing.

Conspirator order: Usurper (Shapeshifter), Cryptkeeper (Grave Digger), Herald (Troublemaker), Soul Broker (Spirit Merchant), Crown Thief (Thief), High Justiciar (Judge). Reactions: Mirror Warden (Mirror Monk), Royal Martyr (Martyr). Dawn: Kingmaker (Mastermind).

Informant inspects a full identity (house and rank), not just allegiance. Oracle additionally sees a uniformly random unplayed hand card. Usurper can include itself among two distinct targets, inspects both, may exchange; both owners lose access after either exchange or keep, per the user’s explicit secrecy rule. Inspected information is a historical snapshot, not live tracking. Herald may publicly reveal the inspected identity. Soul Broker can inspect a seal or identity, then optionally exchange any one owned seal with any one target seal. Crown Thief reveals itself and optionally takes one face-down seal from anyone with more seals (including fallen players). Justiciar reveals itself and executes without reactions. Cutthroat, King's Blade and Justiciar say choose a player and allow self-targeting; the official FAQ specifically permits King's Blade to inspect its own exchanged identity. Mirror Warden redirects death to attacker without creating another reaction window. Martyr grants a seal but still dies. Kingmaker overrides ranks only if alive; Unsworn Kingmaker denies both houses while retaining its own survival reward.

Draft discard contains only the final cards discarded by players, excluding undealt and played cards. Cryptkeeper samples two without replacement; the unchosen remains discarded. Recovered phase cards can play immediately; saving a phase card schedules it at an eligible current/future phase, not an already elapsed phase. Reactions/Dawn must wait for their legal timing.

User customizations: 4–10 players; automatic simultaneous phase reveal (no optional bluff withholding); all identities reveal at dawn; private Prestige totals and values with public seal counts; host explicitly starts each new night. Values are sampled uniformly 2/3/4, fixed on token creation and preserved on transfer. This implementation uses an unbounded seal supply rather than a physical bag. End-night highest total >=10 wins, equal highest totals share coronation. Complete rank-array ties award every survivor; no living claimants means neither house wins and no dead players earn seals.

The card category formerly called Courtier is now **Conspirator**; lowercase courtier means a person. The audited, per-card effect boundaries and 57 isolated contract checks are documented in [CARD_ABILITIES.md](CARD_ABILITIES.md).
